BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · GovTrack · Encyclopedia of Alabama · Washington Post

Barry Moore was born September 26, 1966 in Coffee County, Alabama and grew up on a farm. He attended Enterprise State Community College and Auburn University, graduating with a BS in agricultural science in 1992. While at Auburn he enlisted in the Alabama National Guard. In 1998 he founded Barry Moore Industries, a waste hauling company now known as Hopper-Moore Inc.

State legislature (2010–2018): Moore entered politics in 2010 at the urging of then-Alabama Republican Party chair Mike Hubbard. He was elected to the Alabama House of Representatives, defeating a Democratic incumbent. In April 2014, Moore was arrested for felony perjury and lying to authorities during a grand jury investigation into Hubbard. He was acquitted at trial in 2017.

Congress (2021–present): Moore won Alabama's 2nd District seat in 2020. He is a member of the Freedom Caucus — the House's most conservative bloc. After redistricting following the Supreme Court's Allen v. Milligan ruling (requiring Alabama to create a majority-Black district), Moore was moved into the 1st District and won a primary against fellow incumbent Jerry Carl in 2024.

Town hall incident (August 2025): During a town hall in Daphne, Alabama on August 27, 2025, Moore was booed and heckled by constituents and left abruptly out the back door without completing the event — a notable loss of control in a heavily Republican district.

Senate run (2026): Moore announced in August 2025 he will not seek House reelection and will instead run for Tuberville's Senate seat. Tuberville is running for Alabama governor.

Legislative agenda: Supports abolishing the Department of Education, the IRS (replacing income taxes with a flat national sales tax), and the ATF. Has introduced bills to deport undocumented immigrants charged with DUI, establish term limits, and purchase Greenland. Introduced the "Defeat Sharia Law in America Act."
