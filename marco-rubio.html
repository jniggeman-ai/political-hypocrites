War & Military Action RecordSource: Congress.gov · GovTrack · State Dept. records
Iran — JCPOA Nuclear Deal OppositionOpposed Deal

Rubio was one of the most vocal Senate opponents of the Iran nuclear deal, pushing for its collapse and maximum pressure sanctions. His top donors — Paul Singer, RJC, and the Adelson network — all funded campaigns opposing the deal.

⚠️ Iran did not attack the United States. The JCPOA was a multilateral agreement credited with delaying Iranian nuclear capability. Its collapse accelerated Iran's nuclear program.

Greenland — Did Not Rule Out Military Force (2025)Advocated

As Secretary of State, Rubio stated it was in the U.S. "national interest" to acquire Greenland and explicitly refused to rule out military force during congressional testimony.

⚠️ Greenland is an autonomous territory of Denmark — a NATO ally since 1949. It has not attacked or threatened the United States.

Yemen / Houthi Military Campaign (2025)Supported Strikes

As Secretary of State, Rubio oversaw the re-designation of the Houthis as a Foreign Terrorist Organization and provided diplomatic cover for ongoing U.S. military strikes in Yemen — including strikes that killed civilians.

⚠️ Yemen has not attacked the United States directly. No congressional authorization was obtained for military force in Yemen.

ICC Sanctions — International Court Judges (2025)Sanctioned Judges

Rubio announced U.S. sanctions against ICC judges after the court issued arrest warrants for Israeli PM Netanyahu. First time the U.S. sanctioned judges of an international legal body.

⚠️ Legal scholars from both parties called this an unprecedented assault on international rule of law.
