Peter Thiel's $15 million donation to Vance's 2022 Ohio Senate campaign was the largest single contribution to a Senate candidate in American history.

Thiel hired Vance, mentored him, introduced him to Trump, and lobbied behind the scenes for Vance to become VP. Since inauguration, Thiel's company Palantir's federal contracts have nearly doubled — from $541M to $970M+. Thiel now has a former employee as Vice President of the United States.

Conflicts of Interest & Key Controversies
Palantir / Peter Thiel Federal Contract Expansion
Since Vance became VP, Palantir's federal contracts have nearly doubled — from $541M in 2024 to $970M+ in 2025. Thiel is Palantir's co-founder and Vance's primary career benefactor. Palantir is now being positioned as the structural backbone of entire federal agencies, with a $10B ceiling Army contract. Vance has not recused himself from any government decisions involving Palantir or Thiel-connected companies. Source: Home of the Brave · federal procurement data
Narya Capital — VC Firm Backed by Thiel & Andreessen
Vance founded Narya Capital in 2019 with funding from Peter Thiel, Marc Andreessen, and Eric Schmidt. He retained financial ties to the firm while running for Senate and serving as senator. Both Thiel and Andreessen subsequently became major Trump/Vance donors and now have significant access to the executive branch. Source: OpenSecrets · FEC · SEC filings
"Childless Cat Ladies" Comment
In a 2021 interview, Vance referred to Democratic leaders as "childless cat ladies who don't have a direct stake in the future of the country." The comment went viral during the 2024 campaign. Vance stood by the statement. Critics noted the comment targeted millions of American women voters.
Greenland & Panama Canal Threats
As VP, Vance has echoed and in some cases amplified Trump's rhetoric about acquiring Greenland and the Panama Canal by force if necessary. He visited Greenland in January 2025, framing it as a strategic necessity, drawing formal diplomatic protests from Denmark and Panama. Source: State Dept. · Danish Foreign Ministry
