Background

Early Life & Education

Jared Huffman was born on February 18, 1964 in Independence, Missouri. He graduated magna cum laude from UC Santa Barbara with a B.A. in political science, then earned his J.D. from Boston College Law School.

Pre-Congressional Career

Huffman worked as a consumer attorney and later as a senior attorney at the Natural Resources Defense Council (NRDC), where he focused on water policy and environmental protection. He served on the Marin Municipal Water District board for 12 years before entering electoral politics.

He was elected to the California State Assembly in 2006, representing a Marin County-based district, and served three terms before running for Congress.

Congressional Career

Huffman has represented California's 2nd District — encompassing six North Coast counties from Marin County north to the Oregon border — since 2013. He serves as Ranking Member of the House Natural Resources Committee. He co-founded the Congressional Freethought Caucus, which advocates for secular governance and evidence-based policymaking. Huffman has been publicly open about his secular humanist beliefs, making him one of the few openly non-religious members of Congress.

He is a member of the Congressional Progressive Caucus.

Key Votes

Fiscal Policy · 2023
Voted Against Fiscal Responsibility Act (2023)
Huffman was one of a minority of House Democrats to vote against the Fiscal Responsibility Act of 2023, the bipartisan debt ceiling deal negotiated between Speaker McCarthy and President Biden. Progressive critics of the bill objected to spending caps and work requirements attached to SNAP benefits.
Campus Protest Resolution · November 2023
One of 23 to Vote Against House Resolution on Campus Antisemitism
Huffman was among only 23 House members to vote against a resolution condemning campus support for Hamas and Hezbollah following the October 7, 2023 attacks. His office cited concerns about free speech and the breadth of the resolution's language.
Biden Alignment · 117th Congress
100% Biden Alignment
Voted in alignment with the Biden administration's stated position 100% of the time during the 117th Congress, per GovTrack and ProPublica analysis.
Biden Withdrawal · July 2024
