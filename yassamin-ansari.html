BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · Ansari.house.gov · Ballotpedia · House History

Yassamin Ansari was born April 7, 1992 in Seattle, Washington. She grew up in Scottsdale, Arizona, graduating from Chaparral High School in 2010. She earned a BA from Stanford University in 2014 and an MPhil from the University of Cambridge in 2015. She worked as a staff member for the United Nations from 2015 to 2021 before entering elected politics.

Phoenix City Council: In 2020, Ansari was elected to the Phoenix City Council representing District 7 — a race she won in a runoff. She became the youngest woman ever elected to the Phoenix City Council. She was subsequently selected as Vice Mayor of Phoenix (2023–2024). During her tenure she championed Phoenix's landmark Climate Action Plan, secured millions in free tuition for community college students pursuing trade jobs, and advocated for reproductive rights protection.

Election to Congress: Ansari announced her congressional candidacy in April 2023. She won the August 2024 Democratic primary by just 39 votes over Raquel Terán — one of the closest primary margins in the cycle. She went on to win the general election with nearly 71% of the vote in the deep-blue AZ-3 district. She was elected Democratic Freshman Class President in November 2024 and boycotted Trump's inauguration, attending a Martin Luther King Jr. Day march in Phoenix instead.

Firsts: Ansari is the youngest woman serving in the 119th Congress and the first Iranian American Democrat ever elected to Congress. Her parents are immigrants — her background as the daughter of immigrants has been central to her political identity. Source: Ansari.house.gov · House History

El Salvador trip (April 2025): Ansari joined Democratic colleagues traveling to El Salvador to investigate the conditions of Kilmar Abrego Garcia and other migrants deported there by the Trump administration. Source: Wikipedia
