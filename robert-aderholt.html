BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · GovTrack · Ballotpedia · Aderholt.house.gov

Robert Brown Aderholt was born July 22, 1965 in Haleyville, Alabama. His father was a part-time minister and circuit judge for more than 30 years. Aderholt attended the University of North Alabama, then Birmingham-Southern College where he earned a degree in history and political science, and received his JD from Samford University's Cumberland School of Law. In 1992 he was appointed Haleyville municipal judge. That same year he was a delegate to the Republican National Convention.

Election history: In 1995 Aderholt became the top aide to Alabama Governor Fob James. He won the 1996 Republican primary and defeated State Senator Bob Wilson Jr. 50%–48% — a race that received significant national attention and personal visits from Newt Gingrich. He became only the second Republican to represent the district since Reconstruction. He has been reelected nine times since, running unopposed in 2004, 2010, 2014, and 2016. His district carries a Cook Partisan Voter Index of R+33 — the most Republican congressional district in the United States.

Dean of the delegation: Following Senator Richard Shelby's retirement at the end of the 117th Congress, Aderholt became the dean of Alabama's congressional delegation — the longest-serving member. He has served since January 7, 1997.

Appropriations Committee: Aderholt serves on the House Appropriations Committee and chairs the Subcommittee on Labor, Health and Human Services, and Education — one of the largest appropriations subcommittees. GovTrack reports he has written more laws than any other member of Alabama's delegation, with 10 enacted bills to his name.

Fellowship Foundation: Aderholt is associated with the Fellowship Foundation (also known as "The Family") — a secretive Christian organization that has operated for decades in Washington, D.C. Over 16 years, Aderholt traveled to 18 countries on the Fellowship's behalf, including a 2017 trip to Romania to promote "traditional family values." Source: Wikipedia

Values Action Team: Aderholt chairs the House Values Action Team (VAT) and co-chairs the Congressional Adoption Caucus. He is a signer of Americans for Tax Reform's Taxpayer Protection Pledge.
