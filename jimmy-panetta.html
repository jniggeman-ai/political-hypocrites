Background

Family & Early Life

James "Jimmy" Varni Panetta was born in 1969 in Carmel, California. He is the youngest of three sons of Leon Panetta — who represented this same Central Coast region in Congress from 1977 to 1993, then served as President Clinton's White House Chief of Staff and Budget Director, and under President Obama as CIA Director and Secretary of Defense. His wife, Carrie Panetta, is a judge on the Monterey County Superior Court. They have two daughters who attended the same local public schools Jimmy attended.

Panetta attended Monterey Peninsula Community College before transferring to UC Davis, where he graduated with a degree in International Relations. He earned his law degree from Santa Clara University.

Military Service

Panetta served as a U.S. Navy Intelligence Officer, including a deployment to Afghanistan. His military service included intelligence work in support of combat operations.

Pre-Congressional Career

After the Navy, Panetta worked as a prosecutor in both Monterey and Alameda Counties. He prosecuted criminal cases before running for Congress in 2016 following the retirement of Rep. Sam Farr — who had himself succeeded Leon Panetta in the seat. Democrats have held the seat and its predecessors without interruption since 1977.

Congressional Career

Panetta has served since January 2017 and is now in his fifth term. He serves on the House Ways and Means Committee, the House Budget Committee, and as Chief Deputy Whip in the 119th Congress. His focus areas include agriculture (central to Monterey County's economy), veterans, clean energy, and coastal protection.
