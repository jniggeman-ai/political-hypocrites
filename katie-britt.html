BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · Ballotpedia · Georgetown University · PolitiFact

Katie Britt was born February 2, 1982 in Enterprise, Alabama. She earned a BS in Political Science from the University of Alabama in 2004 and a JD from Alabama Law School in 2013. She was President and CEO of the Business Council of Alabama (2019–2021) and Chief of Staff to Sen. Richard Shelby (2016–2018) — whose seat she ultimately won.

Election: Britt won the 2022 Republican primary against Rep. Mo Brooks, with Trump initially endorsing Brooks before withdrawing and endorsing Britt. She won the general election with 66.6% of the vote — the most lopsided U.S. Senate race in Alabama history. She became the first woman elected to the U.S. Senate from Alabama and the youngest Republican woman ever elected to the Senate at age 40.

2024 SOTU Response: Britt delivered the Republican response to Biden's State of the Union on March 7, 2024 — a widely panned performance filmed from her kitchen in a deliberately domestic setting. She cited a woman who said she was "sex trafficked by cartels starting at the age of 12" as evidence of Biden's border failures. Fact-checkers determined the trafficking occurred in Mexico under the George W. Bush administration — not related to U.S. border policy under Biden. Saturday Night Live parodied the speech with Scarlett Johansson. It became one of the most mocked political speeches in recent memory.

Legislative record: Georgetown's McCourt School ranked Britt the least bipartisan U.S. senator in 2023. She has focused on pro-Israel legislation, child online safety (Kids Off Social Media Act), anti-China land ownership, and border security. She has strongly opposed Ukraine aid while supporting Israel aid.

Israel — Gaza War Military Aid (2024)Voted For

One of the Senate's most consistent Israel supporters. Voted for all Israel aid packages. In her 2022 campaign op-ed she wrote: "Supporting Israel is in America's interest, it's in Alabama's interest, and as a Christian conservative, I believe God commands us to support Israel." In November 2023 she compared the October 7 Hamas attack to September 11 and opposed a ceasefire. Received $102,500+ from AIPAC and affiliated organizations.

Ukraine — Security Supplemental Aid (2024)Voted Against

Voted against continued military and economic aid to Ukraine during the Russian invasion. Part of the Senate Republican bloc that has aligned with MAGA opposition to Ukraine support.

⚠️ Russia invaded and occupies Ukrainian sovereign territory. The U.S. committed to Ukraine's security under the 1994 Budapest Memorandum.
