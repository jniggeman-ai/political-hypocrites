The Story

My name is Julian Niggeman. I was born in November 2000 — which means I've spent my entire conscious life watching some of the most confusing, contradictory, and infuriating events in modern American history unfold in real time.

I grew up in the Philadelphia suburbs not caring about politics at all. Over the past decade, I've done the full circle — every side of this thing. And the one conclusion I've landed on, with total confidence, is this: both parties are completely full of it.

"The two political parties in this country parade around pretending to hate each other while they continue to profit together. They keep us distracted with social movements while they're making money and putting us further into debt."

The information that would let you hold them accountable is all out there. But you have to jump through so many hoops to find it that most people give up. I don't think that's an accident. I think it's designed that way — so you don't look into what they vote for, who gives them money, and what committees they sit on. They'd rather you rely on their speeches and their rallies, where they get you pumped up on a dream that was never going to happen.

Over the past ten years, I've come to believe that absolutely no one in that building is serving the American people. They are serving lobbyists, foreign nations, and themselves. It's time our votes matter again. It's time our voices matter again. And it's time our politicians fight for the American people again — not for the people writing the checks.

I built this site because I'm tired of it — and I think you are too.

The Mission

PoliticalHypocrites.us is a nonpartisan civic transparency platform. The goal is simple: take everything that's already public record and put it in one place, organized, searchable, and free.

Every vote. Every donor. Every stock trade. Every committee assignment. Every broken promise. All sourced. All documented. No spin.

Both parties are tracked equally here. This isn't a left-wing site or a right-wing site. If you're looking for that, you won't find it. What you will find is a record of what politicians actually do — not what they say on television.

The tagline is "United Above, To Distract Below." That's not a slogan. That's the operating model of American politics for the last 50 years.

How to Use This Site

Every politician profile is built the same way. Click any name and you'll find:

Background — career history, key facts, public positions.
Donors — searchable table of who funds them and how much, sourced from FEC and OpenSecrets.
Voting Record — full vote history via GovTrack.
Promises — what they said they'd do, tracked as kept, broken, or mixed.
War Votes — every authorization of military force, broken out separately.
Stock Trades — STOCK Act disclosures. See what they're buying while writing the laws.
Epstein Files — direct links to declassified documents where names appear, or a clear note when they don't.

Use the Donors page to search across the top individual and corporate donors to both parties — with full bios, industry tags, and party breakdowns.

FAQ

Is this site affiliated with any political party?
No. Both parties are tracked equally. The site was built specifically because mainstream political media is almost entirely partisan. This site doesn't frame anything. It just shows the record.
Who funds this site?
Nobody. This is a personal project built and maintained out of pocket. There are no advertisers, no donors, no PAC money, and no political backers. The hosting costs about the same as a Netflix subscription.
How do I know the data is accurate?
Every data point links back to its primary source — FEC.gov, GovTrack, STOCK Act filings, and official government records. You can verify anything on this site yourself in about 30 seconds. That's the point.
Why are there only Republicans on here right now?
The site launched during the current executive administration, so executive branch profiles came first. Congressional profiles — including Democrats — are actively being added. The mission is nonpartisan and the coverage will reflect that.
Can I submit a tip or flag an error?
Yes — see the Contact section below. If you've found a factual error, a missing record, or something that needs to be updated, please reach out. Accuracy matters more than being right.
Can I share content from this site?
All of the underlying data is already public record. Share freely. If you're citing the site directly, a link back is appreciated but not required.
