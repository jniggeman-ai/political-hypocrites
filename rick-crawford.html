BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · Encyclopedia of Arkansas · GovTrack · Ballotpedia

Eric Alan "Rick" Crawford was born January 22, 1966 at Homestead Air Force Base in Florida, the son of an Air Force father. He grew up in a military family and graduated from Alvirne High School in Hudson, New Hampshire. He enlisted in the U.S. Army and served as an explosive ordnance disposal (EOD) technician assigned to the 56th Ordnance Detachment at Fort Indiantown Gap, Pennsylvania — leaving with the rank of Sergeant after four years. He then attended Arkansas State University in Jonesboro, graduating in 1996 with a BS in Agriculture Business and Economics.

Pre-politics career: Crawford was seriously injured in a rodeo accident in 1993 and transitioned into radio announcing for rodeo events. He launched a music career as a "singing cowboy" and in 1994 released an album, "Crackin' Out," dedicated to "that dyin' breed called 'Cowboy'." He became a news anchor and agri-reporter at KAIT-TV in Jonesboro, then launched the AgWatch Network — a farm news network heard on 39 radio stations in Arkansas, Missouri, Tennessee, Mississippi, and Kentucky. Source: Wikipedia · Encyclopedia of Arkansas

Historic election: Crawford was elected in 2010, becoming the first Republican to represent Arkansas's 1st District since Reconstruction — the seat had been in Democratic hands since 1875. He has won every subsequent election with at least 60% of the vote, and ran unopposed in 2020. He is now in his eighth term.

Intelligence Committee: Crawford was elected chair of the House Intelligence Committee for the 119th Congress — a significant elevation for a rural Arkansas congressman. His path to the chairmanship included briefly resigning from the committee in 2019 during the first Trump impeachment to allow another Republican to fill in. Source: Wikipedia · Crawford.house.gov

Novel (2023): Crawford published a spy thriller novel in 2023 titled "The Stinger Proxy." Source: Encyclopedia of Arkansas
