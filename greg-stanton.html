BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · GovTrack · Ballotpedia · Stanton.house.gov

Gregory John Stanton was born March 8, 1970 on Long Island, New York. His family moved to Arizona and he graduated from Cortez High School in west Phoenix in 1988. He earned a BA in History and Political Science magna cum laude from Marquette University in 1992 — where he was a member of Phi Beta Kappa — and a JD from the University of Michigan Law School in 1995. He then worked as an education attorney from 1995 to 2000.

Phoenix City Council and Mayor: Stanton was elected to the Phoenix City Council in 2000 and served until 2009. He was elected Mayor of Phoenix in 2011 and reelected in 2015, serving until 2018 when he ran for Congress. As mayor he oversaw the nation's fifth-largest and one of its fastest-growing cities. He was an early proponent of light rail expansion and urban core development.

Congressional career: Stanton was first elected to Congress in 2018, representing the east Phoenix Valley suburbs. He is now in his fourth term. He sits on the Judiciary Committee and the Science, Space, and Technology Committee. His district covers Tempe, Mesa, and Chandler — home to Arizona State University, several semiconductor manufacturers, and major tech employers.

Biden withdrawal (July 2024): On July 11, 2024, Stanton became one of the early Democratic members of Congress to publicly call on President Joe Biden to withdraw from the 2024 presidential race in the wake of Biden's debate performance. Source: Wikipedia · Ballotpedia

Personal: Stanton is married to Nicole Stanton, an attorney for a cannabis company. They married in 2005, separated in 2016, and were back together by 2019. They have two children. Source: Wikipedia
