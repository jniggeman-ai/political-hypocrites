BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · GovTrack · Ballotpedia · Crane.house.gov

Elijah James Crane was born January 3, 1980 in Tucson, Arizona and raised in Yuma. His father was a pharmacist. He graduated from Cibola High School in 1998 and attended Arizona Western College and the University of Arizona — he did not graduate. He served in the U.S. Navy as a Navy SEAL from 2001 to 2014, a 13-year career.

Bottle Breacher: After leaving the military, Crane co-founded Bottle Breacher, a company producing patriotic-themed bottle openers made from used .50-caliber bullets. The company appeared on Shark Tank in 2014, receiving investment from Mark Cuban and Daymond John. Crane sold the company in 2022 when he ran for Congress. Source: Wikipedia

Election history: Crane ran for Arizona's 2nd Congressional District in 2022 after it was redrawn to be significantly more Republican. He was endorsed by Donald Trump. He defeated Democrat Tom O'Halleran — a three-term incumbent — by 8 points. He won re-election in 2024 against former Navajo Nation President Jonathan Nez. His district covers roughly half of Arizona by land area, spanning from the Four Corners through Coconino and Yavapai counties.

Conspiracy theories: Crane promoted conspiracy theories about both 2024 assassination attempts against Donald Trump — claiming for the first attempt (Pennsylvania) that the gunman did not act alone, and promoting similar theories about the second attempt (Florida). Source: Wikipedia

Judicial impeachment (2025): Crane introduced articles of impeachment against U.S. District Judge Paul Engelmayer for blocking DOGE from accessing Treasury Department financial records of Americans. Elon Musk subsequently donated the maximum allowable amount to Crane and other Republicans who supported the judicial impeachment effort. Source: Wikipedia

Residency: Crane lives in Oro Valley in Pima County — which is part of Arizona's neighboring 6th congressional district, not the 2nd district he represents. Source: Wikipedia
