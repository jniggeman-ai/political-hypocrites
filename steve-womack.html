BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · Womack.house.gov · GovTrack · Ballotpedia

Stephen Allen Womack was born February 18, 1957 in Russellville, Arkansas. He graduated from Russellville High School in 1975 and earned a BA in Communications from Arkansas Tech University in 1979. He served in the Arkansas Army National Guard from 1979 through 2009 — more than 30 years — retiring as a Colonel. His deployment to the Sinai Peninsula in Egypt in 2002 marked the first time a National Guard unit performed the mission of the Multinational Force and Observers. His military awards include the Legion of Merit and the Harry S. Truman Award (the National Guard Bureau's highest honor). Source: Womack.house.gov

Mayor of Rogers (1998–2010): Womack was elected mayor of Rogers, Arkansas in 1998 and served for 12 years. During his mayoralty he sought to address illegal immigration by assigning two INS agents to the Rogers Police Department — a move that prompted a class-action lawsuit by the Mexican American Legal Defense and Educational Fund (MALDEF) alleging racial profiling. He oversaw more than $1 billion in local economic investment. Rogers is part of the booming northwest Arkansas corridor home to Walmart, Tyson Foods, and J.B. Hunt Transport headquarters. Source: Wikipedia

Congressional career: Womack succeeded John Boozman when Boozman ran for Senate. He is now in his eighth term. He chairs the Appropriations Subcommittee on Transportation, Housing, and Urban Development (THUD) and sits on the Defense and Financial Services Appropriations subcommittees. He previously chaired the House Budget Committee (2018–2019). He chaired the West Point Board of Visitors from 2017 and is committed to the Military Academy's mission.

Voting streak: Womack holds one of the longest consecutive voting streaks in House history — casting his 8,823rd consecutive vote in 2025. His career missed vote rate of 0.6% is among the best in Congress. Source: Womack.house.gov · GovTrack
