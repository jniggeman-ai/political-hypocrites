Background

Early Life & Education

Alex Padilla was born in 1973 in Panorama City, Los Angeles. His father immigrated from Mexico and worked as a short-order cook; his mother worked as a housekeeper. Padilla attended MIT, graduating with a degree in mechanical engineering — the first in his family to attend college.

Political Career

Padilla was elected to the Los Angeles City Council in 1999 at age 26, becoming the youngest person elected to the Council at that time. He served as Council President and, on September 11, 2001, was acting Mayor of Los Angeles when the attacks occurred.

He was elected to the California State Senate in 2006, where he served three terms. In 2014, he was elected California Secretary of State — the first Latino to hold that office — and was re-elected in 2018.

On January 20, 2021, Governor Gavin Newsom appointed Padilla to the U.S. Senate seat vacated by Kamala Harris, who became Vice President. Padilla became California's first Latino U.S. Senator. He was subsequently elected to a full term in 2022.

Notable Controversies

No-Bid Contract · 2020
SKDK Contract During 2020 Election
As California Secretary of State, Padilla awarded a no-bid contract to SKDK, a Democratic political consulting firm, to help run voter outreach during the 2020 election cycle. Critics questioned why the contract bypassed the competitive bidding process. SKDK had ties to Democratic campaigns and advocacy organizations.
Incident · June 12, 2025
Forcibly Removed at Kristi Noem Press Conference
Padilla was forcibly removed and briefly handcuffed by Department of Homeland Security agents while attempting to ask questions at a press conference held by DHS Secretary Kristi Noem in Los Angeles. Padilla said he was there as an elected U.S. Senator. DHS said he was not credentialed for the event. The incident drew wide media attention and criticism from civil liberties groups.
