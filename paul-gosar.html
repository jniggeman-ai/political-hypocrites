BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · GovTrack · Ballotpedia

Paul Anthony Gosar was born November 27, 1958 in Rock Springs, Wyoming. He is the eldest of seven sons and three daughters. His paternal grandparents were Slovenian and his maternal grandparents were Basque immigrants. He was raised in Pinedale, Wyoming, and graduated from Pinedale High School in 1977. He earned a BS from Creighton University and a DDS from Creighton University School of Dentistry. He ran a dentistry practice in Flagstaff, Arizona from 1989 to 2010, was named Arizona Dental Association "Dentist of the Year" in 2001, and served as AzDA president from 2004 to 2005. He was first elected to Congress in 2010 — his first run for any elected office.

Family opposition: During Gosar's 2018 re-election campaign, six of his nine siblings appeared in ads for his Democratic opponent, David Brill. His brother Pete is a former chairman of the Wyoming Democratic Party and was a candidate for Wyoming governor. Their mother reportedly referred to Gosar as a "huge embarrassment." Source: Wikipedia

House censure (2021): In November 2021, the House voted 223–207 to formally censure Gosar for posting an animated video on social media that depicted him killing Democratic Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez with a sword and attacking President Biden. It was the first censure of a House member in more than a decade. Republicans largely voted against the censure. Gosar reposted the video after the censure vote. Source: Wikipedia

Far-right connections: Gosar has documented ties to far-right militant groups including the Proud Boys and Oath Keepers. He has connections to Holocaust deniers and has spoken at or associated with white nationalist events and figures. The Arizona Republic describes him as one of the staunchest opponents of legalizing undocumented Dreamers. Source: Wikipedia

Residency questions: When redistricting moved Prescott into the new 2nd district, Gosar claimed a condominium in Bullhead City as his residence in the 9th district — though public records showed he owned a home in Flagstaff. Source: Wikipedia

Dean of delegation: Since Raúl Grijalva's death in March 2025, Gosar and David Schweikert share the deanship of Arizona's congressional delegation. Gosar will become sole dean in 2027 when Schweikert retires to run for governor. Source: Wikipedia
