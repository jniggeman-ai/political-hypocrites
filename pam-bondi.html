Trump University Pay-to-Play — Illegal $25,000 Donation (2013)Confirmed / Illegal

In 2013, while Bondi's office had received at least 22 fraud complaints about Trump University, Bondi personally solicited a $25,000 donation from Donald Trump's charitable foundation. Days after the donation, her office declined to join New York's class action lawsuit against Trump University. Trump's foundation later paid a $2,500 IRS penalty because the donation violated rules against charitable foundations making political donations. The Trump Foundation was ultimately dissolved by court order for systematic misuse of charitable funds.

Source: Tampa Bay Times · IRS records · NY AG Office · Wikipedia

Delayed Execution for Campaign FundraiserConfirmed

As Florida Attorney General, Bondi personally requested a delay in the scheduled execution of convicted murderer Marshall Lee Gore — because the execution date conflicted with one of her own campaign fundraising events. The execution was rescheduled. The Tampa Bay Times reported the headline: "Attorney general vows to never again delay an execution for a fundraiser." Critics called it an extraordinary example of putting political ambition over her legal duties.

Source: Tampa Bay Times · Florida AG records

Qatar Lobbying → Approved $400M Luxury Jet Gift to TrumpActive Conflict

After leaving the Florida AG office, Bondi became a registered lobbyist for the government of Qatar through Ballard Partners. As U.S. Attorney General, she approved Qatar's gift of a $400 million luxury Boeing 747 jet to President Trump — under the condition it be transferred to the Trump Library Foundation. Critics called it an unprecedented conflict of interest: approving a $400M gift from a foreign government she had previously been paid to represent.

Source: Common Cause · OpenSecrets · Ballard Partners disclosures

Epstein Files — Congressional Subpoena (March 2026)Active

A House committee voted to subpoena Bondi in March 2026 over her handling of the DOJ's Epstein file investigation. Democrats stormed out of a DOJ briefing after alleging they were being stonewalled. Bondi has been accused of suppressing or delaying the release of Epstein-related documents. Her handling of the case has become one of the most contentious issues of her tenure.

Source: Britannica · AP · Reuters · March 2026

Pressured Attorneys Investigating Mortgage Fraud to Resign (2011)Documented

In 2011, Bondi pressured two attorneys in Florida's Economic Crime Division to resign. They had been investigating Lender Processing Services (now Black Knight) for "robosigning" — a mortgage fraud scheme that harmed Florida homeowners during the foreclosure crisis. Critics alleged she protected the financial industry at the expense of defrauded homeowners.

Source: Wikipedia · Florida AG records

2020 Election Denial / False ClaimsDocumented

Bondi was one of the loudest voices spreading Trump's false claims that the 2020 election was stolen. She falsely claimed Trump won Pennsylvania. The 2020 election results were certified by Republican secretaries of state, confirmed by 62 court cases, and validated by Trump's own Attorney General William Barr, who said there was "no evidence of widespread fraud." Bondi has not retracted her statements.

Source: Common Cause · Ballotpedia · multiple news outlets

Hosting Fox News Show While Serving as Florida AGEthics Violation Allegation

In August 2018, while still serving as Florida's elected attorney general, Bondi co-hosted "The Five" on Fox News three days in a row. Fox News claimed the Florida Commission on Ethics had approved the appearance. The commission's spokeswoman denied that any decision was made. The Tampa Bay Times called it "unprecedented" for a sitting elected official to host a partisan TV program.

Source: Tampa Bay Times · Florida Commission on Ethics
