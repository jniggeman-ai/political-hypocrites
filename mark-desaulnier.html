Background

Early Life & Career Before Politics

Mark James DeSaulnier was born on March 31, 1952 in Lowell, Massachusetts. He earned a B.A. in History from the College of the Holy Cross. In the early 1970s he moved to California and spent the next three and a half decades as a small business owner, operating and managing several successful Bay Area restaurants including Henry Africa's in San Francisco, Santa Fe Bar and Grill with chef Jeremiah Tower in Berkeley, and TR's in Concord. He was a member of both the Teamsters International Union and the Hotel Employees and Restaurant Employees International Union.

Party Switch

DeSaulnier was a member of the Republican Party until 2000, when he switched to the Democratic Party. He has represented Concord and Contra Costa County in elected office continuously since 1991 — first on the Concord City Council (1991–1994), then as a Contra Costa County Supervisor (1994–2006), as a California State Assembly member (2006–2008), as a California State Senator (2008–2015), and now as a U.S. Representative.

Congressional Career

DeSaulnier was first elected to Congress in 2014, taking office in January 2015. His district — Contra Costa County's East Bay suburbs including Concord, Walnut Creek, Richmond, and surrounding communities — has been renumbered from CA-11 to CA-10 through redistricting. He serves on the Transportation and Infrastructure Committee, the Education and Workforce Committee (where he is Ranking Member of the Health, Employment, Labor, and Pensions Subcommittee), and the Ethics Committee. He is a member of the Congressional Progressive Caucus.

He has passed over 60 legislative efforts since coming to Congress, including the 21st Century Assistive Technology Act and the Contra Costa Canal Transfer Act. In the California legislature, 61 of his bills became state law. He was the first freshman in California Assembly history to chair the Assembly Transportation Committee.

Health — Leukemia Diagnosis & Near-Fatal Hospitalization

Six months after taking office, DeSaulnier was diagnosed with chronic lymphocytic leukemia (CLL) in July 2015 — the most common form of adult blood cancer. He disclosed the diagnosis publicly in May 2016 after completing an initial round of chemotherapy. CLL cannot be cured but can be managed; he has continued to receive oral treatment and has remained in office throughout. He founded the Congressional Cancer Survivors Caucus.

In March 2020, DeSaulnier fell while running near the Capitol, fracturing multiple ribs and injuring his spleen. He developed pneumonia and was hospitalized in intensive care. His condition deteriorated to critical; doctors told his sons his organs were failing and gave him a 10% chance of survival. He was placed on a ventilator for four weeks. He survived and was discharged after nearly two months, calling himself a "medical miracle." He resumed committee work virtually during the early COVID-19 pandemic.

Key Votes

Iran War Powers · 2026
Voted for War Powers Resolution on Iran
DeSaulnier voted for the House War Powers Resolution directing the president to halt unauthorized military hostilities against Iran, stating publicly: "The President has offered no coherent strategy, goals, or endgame for this war, which has already claimed the lives of seven American servicemembers."
Assistive Technology · 2022
Authored 21st Century Assistive Technology Act — Enacted
DeSaulnier authored the 21st Century Assistive Technology Act (H.R. 9028, 117th Congress), which was signed into law. The legislation updated federal programs supporting the development and adoption of assistive technology for people with disabilities.
Transportation
BART Expansion to Eastern Contra Costa County
Before coming to Congress, DeSaulnier provided key legislative leadership for the Highway 4 BART extension to eastern Contra Costa County and the development of a fourth bore of the Caldecott Tunnel — major regional infrastructure projects that shaped his district for decades.
Environment · State Record
California Air Resources Board — Champion of Clean Air Regulations
As a member of the California Air Resources Board, DeSaulnier supported regulations including cleaner-burning gasoline, lower-emission vehicles, identifying diesel exhaust as a toxic air contaminant, banning MTBE from gasoline, classifying secondhand smoke as a carcinogen, and reducing emissions from dairy farms and cruise ships.

War & Foreign Policy

Iran · 2026
Voted for War Powers Resolution — Opposed Trump's Iran Strikes
DeSaulnier voted for the Democratic War Powers Resolution directing the president to remove U.S. forces from unauthorized hostilities in Iran. He publicly criticized the administration for having no coherent strategy and cited the deaths of seven American servicemembers.
General Posture
Progressive Caucus Member — Skeptical of Military Interventionism
As a member of the Congressional Progressive Caucus, DeSaulnier has generally opposed unauthorized military engagements and supported stricter congressional oversight of the use of military force.
