BackgroundSource: Wikipedia · Kelly.senate.gov · Britannica · Phoenix New Times

Mark Edward Kelly was born February 21, 1964 in Orange, New Jersey. His parents both worked for the West Orange Police Department — his father as a police captain, his mother as one of the first women police officers in the town. He attended the U.S. Merchant Marine Academy (BS in Marine Engineering, 1986) and the U.S. Naval Postgraduate School (MS in Aeronautical Engineering). He joined the Navy in 1986, flew 39 combat missions during Operation Desert Storm, and was selected as a NASA astronaut in 1996 in the same class as his identical twin brother Scott Kelly. He flew four Space Shuttle missions — commanding STS-134, the final flight of Space Shuttle Endeavour, in 2011. He spent over 50 days in space total.

Gabby Giffords shooting (January 8, 2011): Kelly's wife, then-Rep. Gabrielle Giffords, was shot in the head during a constituent event in Tucson. Six people were killed and 12 others wounded. Kelly commanded his final space mission just months later, then retired from NASA and the Navy in October 2011 to assist in his wife's recovery. He and Giffords became prominent gun violence prevention advocates, co-founding Americans for Responsible Solutions in 2013 — later renamed the Giffords organization.

Senate career: Kelly was elected in November 2020 in a special election to fill the seat of the late Sen. John McCain, defeating Republican incumbent Martha McSally. He was the first Democrat to hold the seat since 1953. He won a full six-year term in 2022, defeating Blake Masters. He raised $89 million in his 2020 race — one of the most expensive Senate races in history.

2024 VP consideration: Kelly was on Kamala Harris's short list for vice presidential running mate in 2024. Harris ultimately selected Tim Walz. Kelly has since emerged as one of the more vocal critics of the Trump administration's second term, sparring publicly with Pete Hegseth over his fitness for Secretary of Defense and with Elon Musk, whom he called an "asshole" after Musk called him a "traitor" for visiting Ukraine.

Military members video (2025): Kelly appeared in a video reminding military members that they could refuse unlawful orders — which drew criticism from President Trump and Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth. Source: Britannica

War & Foreign Policy RecordSource: Wikipedia · Kelly.senate.gov · Britannica

Operation Desert Storm: Kelly flew 39 combat missions during the Gulf War as a naval aviator. He flew from the aircraft carrier USS Midway. He is a recipient of the Defense Superior Service Medal, Legion of Merit, two Distinguished Flying Crosses, and multiple Air Medals.

Ukraine (2024): Voted for military and economic aid to Ukraine. Has been a vocal supporter of Ukraine against Russian aggression. Was reportedly in contact with Ukrainian officials and visited Ukraine — which led Elon Musk to call him a "traitor," prompting Kelly to call Musk an "asshole" in response. Source: Phoenix New Times

Senate Armed Services Committee: Kelly serves as Ranking Member of the Senate Armed Services Airland Subcommittee, overseeing Army and Air Force equipment and readiness. He has been a consistent advocate for Arizona's military installations and the semiconductor supply chain as a national security issue.

Military orders video (2025): Kelly appeared in a video reminding military members they have the legal right and obligation to refuse unlawful orders — drawing condemnation from President Trump and Secretary of Defense Pete Hegseth. Source: Britannica

Gun violence prevention: Kelly and his wife founded the Giffords organization after the 2011 assassination attempt on Giffords. He has advocated for background checks, red flag laws, and other gun safety measures throughout his Senate career. This is his most prominent non-military policy area.
